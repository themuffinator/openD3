openD3 Interface Atlas

Doom 3's interface, redrawn as vector references from measurement: a cold, self-lit instrument over black space, cut at 45 degrees and traced in glowing hairlines.

Every figure is authored from the numbers in docs/dev/ui-visual-design.md. The page holds no retail art and no retail font data; the planet, the logo and level pictures are labelled stand-ins. Text is set in openD3's own TrueType faces. Values are in u (one unit of the 640x480 canvas; 1 u = 1.5 dp at 1280x720). Controls respond with the measured timings.

Tokens spec 4

The stock furniture is white art tinted where it is drawn, so a colour is a hue, an alpha and a blend. Each family keeps a few hues and spends them across a ladder of alphas. Swatches sit on black, as the interface sits on space.

Families at a glance

Cold chrome, warm world. Steel blue, slate, teal and pale cyan are furniture. Warm colour is the world, danger and the player's own data. A device's additive tint says where it is; red, green-cyan and amber say what state it is in.

Type spec 5

Four faces and one console sheet set every word in the game. Each has a job; sizes cluster tightly; hierarchy comes from the face, the case, the colour and the alpha. The text below is set in openD3's TrueType reconstructions, the same files the engine draws from.

Menu ramp, at reference size

One row per role at 72 x textscale px, the dp size on a 1280x720 view. 323 of the menu's 399 text windows sit at 0.24 to 0.26: a 12 u em. Titles are one step up; nothing exceeds 0.35 outside the credits. No menu text has a shadow, an outline or letter-spacing.

Coverage

The retail atlases cover Latin-1 and draw a hollow box for codes 127 to 159. The TrueType faces draw the Windows-1252 punctuation the stock lacked and extend to Latin Extended, Greek and Cyrillic; this page embeds the Latin subset only.

Chamfer and hairline spec 6.1, 6.2, 6.8, 6.11

Every corner in the menus is a 45 degree cut from a fixed ladder of sizes, and every button edge is a 1 u line of light with a four-step falloff. There is no radius anywhere except one concave arc in the top-right flare.

Controls spec 6.3 to 6.7, 7.1 to 7.5

State is light. A control at rest is dim; under the pointer or keyboard focus its hairline flashes and settles brighter and its label turns white; a press is a flash that decays. Nothing moves, grows or holds a pressed pose. Every control below is live and drawn in u on a page-plate ground.

Plates and dialogs spec 6.9, 6.10, 9.9

A page sits on a translucent slate polygon tied to the nav band by a bridge and loose strokes; it has no outline, and the scene shows through it. A dialog is the one opaque object: a plate with four cut corners inside a separate outline of light, over a dimmed scene.

Construction spec 8.1 to 8.7

The menus behave like hardware switching on. Panels unfold out of the nav band, every piece is born as a flash of white light that settles to its tint in two stages, pieces fire 50 ms apart, and text appears last in a single frame. Colour moves linearly; geometry decelerates; nothing overshoots.

HUD spec 10.1 to 10.8, 10.10

The marine's suit display: two slanted corner plates on an otherwise empty view, bare numbers, and notices that assemble themselves and leave. Every stroke is translucent grey-green on a 400 ms flicker; a warning changes the hue of the same art.

PDA spec 10.9

A full-screen, opaque device that boots: chrome bands part from the middle, panels slide in from all four edges and fields wipe in row by row. White text at stepped alphas on near-black under a 15 u grid; orange only on objective titles.

Devices spec 11.1 to 11.9

457 screens are mapped onto level surfaces and used by looking and firing. Each is a UAC product with its own layout, built from one hardware vocabulary: a dark tube, a stack of near-subliminal monitor artefacts, slab-and-hairline frames and one status language. Colour is state; tint is place.

Multiplayer spec 10.11 to 10.13

One teal hairline family over the live game: a tab strip with a panel hanging from the current tab, two slanted plates with sheared numerals, and a table that snaps shut around its rows. Outlines flicker between 100% and 82% of their alpha every 400 ms; warnings change the tint of the same art.

Loading and console spec 10.14, 10.15

A nearly black picture under two cut bands, one word typed out above an amber bar, and the level's name below. Then the plainest part of the interface: fixed cells of cyan text on black.

openD3 Interface Atlas, drawn from visual specification 1.0. Doom 3 is id Software's; this page reproduces none of its art. The five typefaces are openD3's TrueType reconstructions of the retail bitmap fonts, embedded as Latin subsets.

u = one unit of the stock 640x480 canvas · dp = density-independent pixel at the 1280x720 reference · accel(a, d) = the stock transition's acceleration and deceleration fractions
